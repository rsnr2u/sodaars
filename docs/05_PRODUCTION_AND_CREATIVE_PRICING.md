# Production, Mounting & Creative Rate Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary & Concept Alignment

The outdoor advertising (OOH) pricing system has been unified to accurately reflect physical vs concept-driven production economics:

1. **Area-Driven Physical Rates (`sq.ft × ₹/sq.ft`)**:
   - **Printing Charges**: Standard ₹12/sq.ft (Heavy-duty 440 GSM UV-solvent vinyl).
   - **Mounting Charges**: Standard ₹5/sq.ft (Certified rigger installation squad + electrical tensioning audit).
   - *Example*: For an 18×18 ft hoarding (324 sq.ft), printing = ₹3,888 and mounting = ₹1,620.
2. **Concept-Driven Creative Economics (Master Campaign Resizing)**:
   - Creative concept design is conceptual (1 master artwork adapted across different billboard sizes).
   - In OOH, providers use the creative fee as a high-conversion sales incentive: **Courtesy Discount Waiver** (`~₹3,000~ ₹0`), showing transparency while closing deals without eroding media rental margins.
3. **Provider-Configured Global Defaults in Settings**:
   - Configurable in **Settings $\rightarrow$ Finance & Commercials** tab, with live sample preview calculations.
4. **End-to-End Propagation**:
   - **Settings Rate Cards** $\rightarrow$ **Asset Overrides** $\rightarrow$ **Direct Booking Modal & 4-Step Builder** $\rightarrow$ **Official Proforma Quotation / Release Order (RO)**.
